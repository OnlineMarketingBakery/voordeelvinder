## 3. Legal pages

The designer added full-looking drafts of the **Algemene voorwaarden** (134:2705) and the
**Privacybeleid** (140:3318). There is no Cookiebeleid frame. The drafts are generic template
text, not lawyer-drafted, and they contradict the build:

- **The model:** they describe an energy-only "tussenpersoon" and switching service paid by a
  supplier fee ("no cure, no pay"), with on-screen results. The partner callback and lead
  sharing are never mentioned.
- **The company:** no legal entity, KBO/BTW or address, and three different mailboxes.
- **The privacy page in particular:**
  - it shares data "alleen" with "de leverancier van jouw keuze";
  - its data list has no phone number;
  - its retention periods (12 months / 24 months / 7 years) don't match the pipeline (30-day
    server backup, 24 months long-term);
  - its cookie section contradicts itself and has no marketing category (Meta Pixel);
  - it doesn't mention transfers outside the EU (Meta, Google, Microsoft, Mailchimp,
    Cloudflare) or a complaint route to the GBA/APD;
  - it doesn't mention the automated promo / no promo classification.
- **The terms** say they apply "uitsluitend … op particuliere gebruikers", but the flow accepts
  business addresses (§7.3).
